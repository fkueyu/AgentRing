AgentRing round enclosure V2 test-fit notes
中文: 打印与装配.txt

Assembled outer diameter 71.60 mm, total height 15.42 mm. Fits the measured
64.00 mm diameter, 1.60 mm thick ring board; the top of the LEDs to the bottom
of the USB-C metal shell is 6.50 mm.

The three STLs are exported in millimetres and in the actual print orientation.
Import them as separate parts into the slicer and keep 100% scale.
01_Base_V2 is the base, 03_Center the centre cap; either can be dark PLA or
PETG. 02_Diffuser is the one-piece translucent ring; use a milky translucent
filament. Ordinary opaque white filament may block the light.
Start the first print with a 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.20 mm layer height. The base
bottom faces the bed; the visible faces of the diffuser and centre cap face the
bed. The top of the USB-C opening in the base needs a bridge of about 10 mm;
check it in the slicer preview.

Test-fit order
1. Print the base first. Tilt the bare board's USB-C end slightly into the side
   opening, then let the board sit on the ring ledge with the LEDs facing up.
   Check that the board is stable, then plug in a known-good cable all the way.
2. Fit the diffuser; align its side key with the groove in the base. Make sure
   the three small stops sit between LEDs. Press gently around the
   circumference. If it touches an LED or needs force, take it out and check.
3. Align the small key on the centre cap with the diffuser's inner groove and
   press it in. The two small holes are for RST and BOOT; use a thin plastic
   rod. The seam in the base has a pry notch for opening.

Design clearances
PCB seat diameter 64.60 mm; USB-C side opening 12.0 × 8.2 mm; two button holes
2.20 mm diameter.
The measured 6.50 mm total thickness is split in the model as 1.70 mm LED
height, 1.60 mm board thickness and 3.20 mm USB-C height. The protrusions on
each side, button coordinates and LED positions are estimated from photos. The
distance from the LEDs to the inner top of the diffuser is about 4.11 mm.
Confirm the snap fit, plug-shell clearance and light diffusion on the physical
part.

V2 changes: the opening is centred on the original centre, 1 mm wider at the
top and bottom, 1 mm narrower on each side, with C1.0 × 45° corners. The clear
width between the two anti-rotation ribs inside is 9.94 mm. When fitting the
board, centre the metal connector between the ribs so it does not crush the
solder joints.

The Blender project contains the assembly scene and an exploded preview scene.
The PCB and electronics are for alignment only. Print the three separate STLs.
